About Me

I entered cybersecurity in 2019 after being hired at a small consulting firm based on the South Shore of Massachusetts, OCD Tech, having just graduated from the University of Massachusetts Boston with an English degree. I was an entry level analyst assigned solely to a privileged access management program for an international bank, reconciling 1000s of Linux servers daily. After automating a large portion of that process with Python (in the pre-AI days), I was promoted to the penetration testing team.

In the years that have followed, I have acquired industry certifications, uncovered critical vulnerabilities (many of which are locked behind clients’ NDAs), registered CVEs, scraped 1 billion robots.txt entries from the worldwide web, presented at conferences, and had my work published in hacking magazines. I have grown, led, and mentored penetration testing teams, even spanning international borders.

I specialize in internal, external, web application, mobile application, and wireless penetration testing, along with social engineering and red teaming. Tangentially, I dabble in the RF space as both a licensed amateur radio operator (KD1AGW) and a satellite enthusiast. I am happy to share my projects, original writing, and research in this portfolio.
